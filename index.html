BOYENSENG 24/7 is a one-stop digital marketplace for all water-related services. It connects customers with verified drilling companies, pump installers, maintenance technicians, and other water solution providers. Instead of just being a "borehole app," it offers a complete ecosystem including drilling, pump installation, solar pumping, water testing, tank installation, farm irrigation setup, spare parts, insurance, and ongoing maintenance.
The biggest risk with borehole drilling is trust. Most people drill only once in their lifetime, so the key question is not "who is cheapest?" but "who can I trust with my money?" This platform solves that by:
  1. Showing you only verified contractors with proven track records.
  2. Providing transparent profiles with experience, success rates, customer reviews, photos, and videos.
  3. Holding your payment securely and releasing it in stages only when agreed milestones are completed.
  4. Protecting you if a driller damages your property, fails to find water, or disappears.
Contractors cannot simply register without approval. Every contractor must submit:

Business registration documents
  1. National ID
  2. Photos of their equipment
  3. Records of previous completed projects
  4. Professional references
Once verified, they receive a trust badge such as:

Verified Driller
  1. Premium Partner ★★★
  2. Elite Contractor ★★★★

This ensures only reliable, qualified professionals appear on the platform.
The platform charges a 10% commission on the total amount of every material purchased or every contractor job completed through the marketplace. This means the platform earns revenue only when a successful transaction occurs, aligning its interests with both customers and contractors.
